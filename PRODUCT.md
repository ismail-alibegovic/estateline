## Register

Product (app UI / admin dashboard / tool). Design serves function.

## Users & Purpose

Real estate agents and agency managers in Bosnia, Croatia, Serbia, Montenegro. Daily operational CRM: property listings, lead tracking, deal pipeline, invoicing, analytics.

## Anti-References

Generic AI dashboard templates (card grids with random icons), warm cream/sand AI defaults, emoji icons, generic "coming soon" placeholders.

## Brand Personality

Premium, dark-tech, geometric, bold purple accent (#3520D5), zero emoji. Minimalist but authoritative — production-grade B2B SaaS.

## Design Principles

- Clean spacing, refined typography (DM Sans / geometric sans)
- Dark backgrounds preferred, bold accent, geometric placeholders
- No placeholder content — every module must feel intentional
